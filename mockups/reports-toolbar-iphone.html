What to check on the phone:
  • The toolbar title and Export button share the first row, and the three layout tabs share the second. No control is clipped.
  • Period chips wrap to two rows; the four selects sit two per row, and the date range closes the filter block.
  • KPIs are 2 across instead of stacked four high, so the first chart starts within the first screen.
  • Charts scale down to the card width. There is no sideways scrolling.
  • The toolbar scrolls away with the page. If you want it pinned while scrolling, that is a separate choice and costs about 90px of viewport.
