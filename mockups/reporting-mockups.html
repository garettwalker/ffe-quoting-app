What the three options share: the same data model the app already has (quotes, invoices, payments, emails, job dates). Every filter recalculates the headline numbers, the charts, and the tables together. The difference is how much control is on the surface: option 1 is a fixed weekly view, option 2 is built around period comparison, and option 3 is a configurable report with saved views.
