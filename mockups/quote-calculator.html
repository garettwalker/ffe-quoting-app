A · Floating calculator

A small round button sits in the corner of the builder. Tap it and the calculator opens above it; on a phone it becomes a bottom sheet.

  • Always one tap away, never in the way
  • Can write the result into whichever field you last tapped
  • Same widget on desktop and phone
Recommended

B · Calculator in the totals rail

The calculator is a card under the Live Total, visible the whole time you are on the page.

  • No overlay or extra tap to open
  • Easy to glance at while typing
  • Makes the rail long, and on a phone it sits below the form

C · Calculator on the field

A small calculator icon next to Qty and Unit Price opens a calculator tied to that field and applies the result back.

  • Most direct: the math lands where it is used
  • Good for area-to-quantity and price math
  • More taps, and more icons on an already busy row

What the calculator needs to do: basic arithmetic, percent (for markup, tax, deposit), and an area mode that takes feet and inches with an optional waste percentage. A tape line of the last few calculations is useful when pricing several lines in a row. Any result is a draft until you press Use, so it can never change a quote by accident.
