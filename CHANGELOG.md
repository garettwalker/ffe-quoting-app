# Changelog

Newest first. The README holds the current routes, data model, and pending list; this file holds the dated work history and the archive of completed capabilities.

## 2026-10-28

- **Typography: Inter Tight everywhere.** The app previously paired Georgia headings with Inter body text. Georgia's literary curves read whimsical on quotes and invoices, so the whole UI now uses Inter Tight (headings and body) as a single family, self-hosted through `next/font/google` and exposed as `--font-inter-tight`. Tailwind's `body` and `display` families and `globals.css` point at the same stack. The PDF headings changed from Times-Bold to Helvetica-Bold so the printed documents are sans-only too. Design references: `mockups/font-options.html` and `mockups/sans-options.html`.
- **Receivables: the period filter now drives the headline totals.** The three summary cards (Total Invoiced, Total Outstanding, Total Paid) used to compute from every job regardless of the selected period. They now live inside `components/receivables-table.tsx`, computed from the filtered job list, so changing the period updates the numbers and the tables together. The old "Outstanding in period" note was replaced with a count of the jobs shown.
- **README split.** The dated history and the detailed completed-work archive moved to this file. The README now holds the current model, routes, rules, and a short pending list.
- **Reports page built at `/reports`.** All three mockup layouts are now real and switchable: **One screen** (filters, KPIs, monthly invoiced/collected, AR aging, quarter-over-quarter, top customers, quote-to-paid), **Compare first** (this period against the prior by month, quarter table, customer movement, job-type mix), and **Report builder** (rail filters, saved views, chart/table toggle). Filters cover period presets plus a custom range, group by month/quarter/year, compare prior year/quarter/month, customer, and job type; every filter recomputes the KPIs, charts, and tables. Invoiced uses the receivable date (emailed or paid), collected uses the payment date, outstanding uses the invoice balance, so a partial payment and a scheduled finish both land correctly. Data is flattened server-side in `lib/reports-server.ts` (quotes + receivable invoices + settled payments, with a synthetic payment for legacy invoices that predate the ledger); all aggregation lives in client-safe `lib/reports.ts`; charts are hand-built SVG in `components/reports/report-charts.tsx`; Export CSV writes the selected grouping. Added a Reports entry to the Money nav group. The report builder's four starter views are now real **saved views**: name the current filter set, save it, click a view to restore it, reuse a name to update it, or delete it. They persist in `localStorage` for this browser and device (four defaults seed on first open); a shared `report_views` table is the next step if the same list is needed on the phone and desktop.
- **Design mockups added.** `mockups/font-options.html` (serif vs sans pairings), `mockups/sans-options.html` (sans families with a heading-weight control), and `mockups/reporting-mockups.html` (three reporting layouts on sample data). These are static reference files, not part of the Next.js build.

- 2026-09-29: Manual partial payments on any invoice. Chad collected $15,000 against an invoice on a stalled job and the app had no way to record it: an invoice was either **unpaid** or **paid** (binary flag), so a one-off partial had nowhere to go — marking it paid overstated what came in, leaving it unpaid understated it, and neither left a record of the money. New helpers `invoicePaidCents` / `invoiceBalanceCents` / `invoiceIsPartial` / `invoiceIsOverpaid` / `quoteCollectedCents` / `withInvoicePaidCents` / `invoiceStatusFor`; `outstandingCents`, `invoiceOutstandingCents`, `invoiceIsReceivable` and the `computeInvoiceAmounts` freeze trigger (was `status === "paid"`, now "has any payment") became paidCents-aware, so `/receivables`, the dashboard tiles, the Customers Quoted/Invoiced/Paid figures and `/projects` followed automatically with no changes of their own. **UI**: new `components/invoice-payment-controls.tsx` replaces `components/invoice-paid-button.tsx` (deleted) on the invoice card — a progress line ("Paid $15,000.00 of $30,945.50, $15,945.50 due") with a bar, the payment history (amount, date, method, note, who recorded it), **Record payment** (amount defaulting to the balance, date received, optional note, live "New balance" preview), **Mark paid in full** (records the remainder as one entry so every dollar has a ledger row), and **Clear payments** (was Mark Unpaid); a manual row has Remove, a Stripe row says "Reverse in Stripe" and is never deleted from the app. Recording by hand is blocked while an online payment is still processing so the same money can't be counted twice, and Clear payments is blocked outright once real card/ACH money has settled. `components/status-badge.tsx` `InvoicePaidBadge` gained a **Partial** state. **Amount changes keep the money** (all three builders): a finish/final/service invoice with money collected keeps its `paidCents`, recomputes status, and warns with the new balance before saving instead of the old reset-to-unpaid (which also orphaned its ledger row); an amount **below** what was collected is allowed and flagged "Overpaid by $X".
